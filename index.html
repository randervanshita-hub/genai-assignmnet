What Pehli does not do

  • Connect to your bank or read your SMS.
  • Ask you to log expenses.
  • Give investment, tax or legal advice, or recommend any financial product.
  • Store your name, email or phone number.

What it does

  • Turns one salary into a weekly and daily figure you can check before you pay.
  • Treats money sent home as a commitment, not as leftover.
  • Keeps a 10% cushion so one surprise does not break the month.
  • Shows the two flexible categories where a small trim adds the most back.
